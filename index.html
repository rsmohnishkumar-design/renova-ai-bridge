Write one task, hand it to an assistant, bring the answer back, and keep the whole discussion in one place. Free, private, and no account needed.

Copy-and-paste workspace: this page does not connect to ChatGPT, Claude or any other AI service, and it needs no API key. You move the text between assistants yourself.

How it works

  1. Describe the task. Save a shared brief below.
  2. Hand off. Copy the handoff and paste it into your assistant's own chat.
  3. Bring it back. Paste the answer here, then copy it to the next assistant for review.
