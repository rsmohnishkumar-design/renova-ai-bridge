    Simulated AI development team

    Type an app idea and a scripted "team" discusses it in four roles: ChatGPT (Architect & Reviewer), Claude (Lead Developer), Gemini (Design & Innovation Reviewer) and Grok (Critical Reviewer). The text is generated locally by this page from your idea, focus and requirements using fixed rules and templates. No real AI service is connected, and none of these assistants actually took part.

      Privacy and storage

      Your task, notes, prompts and saved responses are stored only in this browser (localStorage) on this device. Nothing is sent to any server, there is no account, and there are no API keys. Other browsers or devices will not see it, and clearing site data removes it. Use Clear Conversation to delete saved messages, or Reset Workspace to delete everything. Don't paste passwords or confidential information.

      Not supported: automatic messaging with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok. This tool deliberately has no such feature.
